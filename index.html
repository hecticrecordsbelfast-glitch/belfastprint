Film transfer (DTF)
Your design is printed onto a special film, then heat-pressed onto the shirt. Vivid colour, works on any fabric, and holds up well to washing.
Direct to garment (DTG)
Your design is printed straight onto the fabric, like an inkjet printer for t-shirts. Gives a softer feel for detailed, photo-style designs.
